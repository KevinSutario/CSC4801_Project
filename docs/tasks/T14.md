## Goal
A candidate applies to an open job through a confirm screen that shows exactly what will be shared,
and can see all their applications. A second pending application to the same job is refused.

## Requirement
FP-CAN-3: pending duplicate and application owner. Rule text (brief section "3. Candidate
workflow", FP-CAN-3 "Applications"):
> A candidate **MUST** be able to apply to a job once. A new application **MUST** have status `Pending`. A duplicate application by the same candidate to the same job **MUST** be rejected without creating another row. A candidate **MUST** be able to view the job, employer, current status, and interview booking (if any) for each of their own applications.
>
> A candidate **MUST NOT** be able to view another candidate's applications or change an application status.

Status list (brief section "4. Employer workflow", FP-EMP-3 "Applicant management"):
> The employer who owns the job **MUST** be able to set an application to one of exactly these statuses: `Pending`, `Interviewing`, `Rejected`, or `Accepted`. Other employers and candidates **MUST NOT** be able to change the status.

Decided (D11):
1. Statuses: exactly four, stored lower-case `("pending", "interviewing", "rejected", "accepted")`,
   labels `Pending`, `Interviewing`, `Rejected`, `Accepted`. FP-SCHED-2 keys eligibility on the
   literal `interviewing`.
2. Apply once ever: any existing application to the same job (whatever its status) is rejected with
   `Conflict`, without creating a row. The unique index is `uq_application (job_id, candidate_id)`
   with no status clause. Re-applying after a rejection is refused.
3. No transition rules (not in brief): the owning employer may set any status other than the
   current one.

DEFAULT (not in brief): duplicate gives `Conflict("You have already applied to this job.")` and
HTTP 409; cover note max 2000 chars. The resume is private to the candidate and is NOT attached to
an application (D10): `apply` has no `resume_id` and the confirm page has no resume select.

Another candidate's application (D9): `get_own_application` raises `Forbidden` (a missing one is
`NotFound`).

## Contract
The `applications` T14 parts of `07-CONTRACTS.md`. Routes: `applications.confirm` GET and
`applications.submit` POST `/jobs/<int:job_id>/apply`, `applications.mine` GET
`/candidate/applications`, all `@role_required("candidate")`.

## Steps
1. Tests first.
2. `apply`: job must exist (`get_job`; missing: `NotFound`). Check for any existing application to
   the same job first (friendly `Conflict`), then insert inside `transaction(conn, immediate=True)`
   and also catch `sqlite3.IntegrityError` from `uq_application` as the same `Conflict` (two clicks
   at once). Audit `application.submit`.
3. `get_own_application`: by id and candidate id; `NotFound` if missing, `Forbidden` if another
   candidate's.
4. `list_for_candidate`: one query joining jobs and companies (and the booking, if any), newest
   first.
5. Confirm page uses `confirm_box`: the job and company, cover note, and "This will be sent to
   <company>: your name, your skills, your cover note. Not shared: your resume, your email address,
   your other applications." Primary button "Submit application". Every POST form includes
   `csrf_field()`.
6. Submit: success redirects to `/candidate/applications` with flash "Application sent to <company>";
   `Conflict` re-renders the confirm page with the message and status 409.
7. Mine page: table with job, company, date, status badge (`LABELS`), the booking time when there is
   one, link to `/candidate/applications/<id>` (T16 builds it).
