# T27 · Show interview notes and meeting links after booking

| Phase | Lane | Depends on | Joins lanes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Scheduling + Candidate + Employer | CC03, T12, T14, T15, T16, T17 | yes | BLOCKED on CC03 |

## Goal

An employer can provide a room, instructions, or a meeting link when creating a slot. The
candidate can open the booked interview details from My applications. The employer can see
the booked candidate and job in Interview slots and the booking note in Applicants.

## Requirement

FP-SCHED-2 requires the booking to be visible to the candidate and owning employer. Notes are
a P2-approved enhancement. The candidate sees the owning employer's note after booking.
Only well-formed standalone HTTPS URLs with a host become **Join interview** links. Other note
lines render as escaped text.

## Contract

Use the slot and booking view contracts recorded by CC03. The employer applicants route
provides notes separately from the existing applicant records. Slot selection and booking
confirmation do not include the note.
